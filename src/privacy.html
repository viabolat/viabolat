Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how viabolat ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit or interact with viabolat.com (the "Service").

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the Service and deliver diagnostic and automation-related work. This may include:

  • Email address and basic contact details when you reach out or request a diagnostic
  • Information you voluntarily provide related to datasets, workflows, or systems for diagnostic purposes
  • Limited, anonymized website usage data (e.g., page views) via basic analytics

How We Use Information

Information is used strictly to:

  • Respond to inquiries and diagnostic requests
  • Perform scoped diagnostic analysis or agreed-upon implementation work
  • Improve the clarity and reliability of the Service

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or diagnostic-related data.

Diagnostic Data Handling

Any data shared for diagnostic purposes is:

  • Used solely for the agreed engagement
  • Accessed only for analysis and reporting
  • Not reused for other clients or internal products
  • Deleted or returned upon request, unless retention is required by law

Cookies & Analytics

We may use minimal analytics to understand general website usage. These tools do not identify you personally and are used only to improve the Service.

Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or update your information
  • Request deletion of your data

Requests can be made at any time by contacting us via email.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us directly.
