Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

viabolat is run by Ahmet Bolat ("I", "me"), who is responsible for the personal data described here. This policy explains what I collect when you visit viabolat.com or work with me, why, and what you can ask me to do with it.

What I collect

  • Contact details you send me by email or through the enquiry form: name, email, company and what you describe.
  • Payment details when you book a Check. Payment is handled by Stripe, so I see your name, email and payment status, never your card number.
  • Data in your systems that I see while doing agreed work, such as records in your CRM, forms or spreadsheets.
  • Basic technical data, such as IP address and browser, which the hosting and security providers log when anyone visits a website.

How I use it

  • To reply to you, quote, deliver the agreed work, and invoice.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

I don't sell, rent or share your data for marketing, and I don't send newsletters you didn't ask for.

Data from your systems

Anything I can see in your systems while working for you is:

  • Used only for the agreed work
  • Not copied out of your systems except where the work requires it, such as an example in a report
  • Never reused for other clients or my own products
  • Deleted or returned on request, and deleted from my side when the engagement ends, unless the law requires me to keep it

You can remove my access to your tools at any time.

Services that process data for me

  • Stripe: payments
  • Google (Google Forms): enquiry form responses
  • Google (Tag Manager and Analytics): visitor statistics for this website
  • Cloudflare and HostGator: website security and hosting
  • My email provider: email correspondence

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics, which counts visits and shows which pages are read and how people arrive. Google Analytics sets cookies and records details such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser. I use these reports only to improve the site. I don't use advertising cookies, and I don't sell or share this data for advertising.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google Analytics runs without cookies: it sends anonymous signals that can't identify you or link your visits together, and nothing is stored on your device for analytics.

You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. Google's own use of the data is described in its privacy policy.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become projects: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Invoices and payment records: as long as tax law requires.
  • Data from your systems: until the engagement ends, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal data I hold about you, or object to how I use it. Email contact@viabolat.com and I'll respond within 30 days. If you're in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Romania, that is the ANSPDCP.

Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access only through your own account invites where the platform allows it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Contact

Questions about your data: contact@viabolat.com.
