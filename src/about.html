Most data loss isn't a hack.
It's a broken handoff nobody is watching.

A form that stopped sending. A sync that overwrites the wrong field. An automation whose builder moved on. viabolat finds where your data is leaking and stops it.

Here's what usually happens

A business connects its tools: a website form to a CRM, the CRM to invoicing, a Zapier or Make workflow, a Python script, an API integration. It works great for a few months.

Then something changes. An API updates. A field name shifts. A token expires. The connection still looks healthy, but records stop arriving, or arrive wrong.

Nobody notices right away. A lead never gets a reply. An invoice never goes out. Someone on your team starts patching records by hand, quietly, without telling anyone.

By the time it surfaces, weeks of leads and records are gone. And the person who built it? Unavailable, expensive to re-engage, or just gone.

That's the gap viabolat fills.
